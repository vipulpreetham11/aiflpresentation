The Problem

Current Fraud Detection Isn't Enough

Traditional checks

Traditional authentication verifies identity, not financial intent. Even when all security checks pass, customers can still be manipulated into authorizing fraudulent payments.

Real attacker

Attackers exploit human trust instead of technical vulnerabilities. The customer becomes the attacker's tool, making the payment appear completely legitimate.

Approval logic

Most payment systems stop after authentication. Once identity is verified, the transaction is usually approved without evaluating whether the payment matches the customer's normal financial behaviour.

Common scams

These scams trick users into transferring money directly. Examples include investment fraud, fake customer support, fake KYC updates, and digital arrest scams.

The customer completes authentication — but the intent is coerced. Detection needs context, not just credentials.

Architecture

How AFIL Thinks

The complete intelligence pipeline, evaluating intent in milliseconds.

1. Payment

The trigger. A transaction request arrives in real-time.

2. Knowledge Engine

Because decisions require context. It instantly loads the historical state of the user, device, and merchant.

3. Feature Engineering

Because raw data is useless. It transforms timestamps and amounts into behavioural velocities and graph connections.

4. Seven Intelligence Engines

Because no single model sees the whole picture. Specialized AI evaluates different angles simultaneously.

5. Evidence Fusion

Because AI outputs are incompatible. It normalizes probabilities, anomalies, and graph scores into a universal language.

6. Consensus

Because opinions must be weighed. It calculates trust based on which engines are historically accurate.

7. Conflict Resolution

Because AI disagrees. It gracefully handles edge cases where engines provide conflicting evidence.

8. Business Policy

Because humans need the final say. Deterministic rules act as an emergency brake for extreme anomalies.

9. Decision

The verdict. Approved, Denied, or Challenged—delivered with full explainability.

Multi-Agent AI

Implementation

The Engineering Effort

What we overcame to build this system in 36 hours.

Real-time Knowledge Engine

Graph traversals usually take seconds. We built an in-memory architecture to fetch historical context in under 10ms to meet payment SLAs.

Coordinating Seven Models

Running complex models sequentially causes unacceptable latency. We implemented a highly concurrent orchestration layer where models fire asynchronously.

Evidence Normalization

How do you compare an anomaly score (-1 to 1) with a probability (0 to 1) and a PageRank metric? We engineered a mathematical fusion layer into a unified vector space.

Explainability

Banks cannot trust black boxes. AFIL translates raw scores into human-readable narratives, explaining exactly which engines agreed and which conflicted.

Replay Architecture

Data science requires "time travel". We built a mechanism to accurately reconstruct the exact database state and graph from months ago to replay transactions.
